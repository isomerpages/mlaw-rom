<p>Dear licensed / exempt moneylender,</p>
<p></p>
<p>The Financial Action Task Force (“**FATF**”) published a peer evaluation report
of Singapore, which strongly affirmed that Singapore has a robust and effective framework and process to counter money-laundering (“**ML**”), terrorism financing (“**TF**”) and proliferation financing (“**PF**”).</p>
<p></p>
<p>Licensed and exempt moneylenders may refer to the [press release](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/singapore-has-a-robust-framework-for-combatting-financial-crime-according-to-international-body) jointly published by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (“**MAS**”), the Ministry of Home Affairs (“**MHA**”), and the Ministry of Finance (“**MOF**”).</p>
<p></p>
<p>The [published report](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/content/fatf-gafi/en/publications/Mutualevaluations/mer-singapore-2026.html) is available on the FATF website. The report has also been uploaded at the [ROM website](https://rom.mlaw.gov.sg/information-for-moneylenders/anti-money-laundering-and-countering-the-financing-of-terrorism/).</p>
<p></p>
<p>The fight against ML/TF/PF is a shared responsibility. We thank you for
your continued support and partnership to keep Singapore’s AML/CFT/CPF
regime strong and trusted.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Thank you.</p>
